# Evergreen civic and group quest concepts (not released editions)

**Status: concept library, not purchasable, playable, licensed or route-verified packs.**
The [current canonical pack specification](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/quest-authoring/blob/main/SPEC.md) requires exactly six stops, three hints per stop, and validated content. This file provides original **story briefs**, not manifest JSON or an event release.

## 1. City stories: six chapters

Audience: a visitor center, cultural organization or downtown group. Focus on a fictional city's changes over time rather than a fabricated historical claim about a real place. Possible chapters: welcome, transportation, architecture, public art, local ecosystems, shared finale. A city edition requires locally reviewed sources, permission for signs/public art, an accessible route and dated field tests. No city is advertised as available by this concept.

## 2. Neighborhood pride

Audience: an HOA, library or neighborhood group. An original story with six collaborative reflection prompts: community welcome, shared greenspace, everyday helpers, local creativity, safe route feature, shared finale. Collect stories by consent, not by taking photos of private properties or disclosing residents' locations. Avoid sensitive imagery and minors' information.

## 3. Workplace / nonprofit cooperation

Audience: corporate or volunteer groups. Original neutral story with six cooperative problem-solving activities, designed for one phone per team with hints and alternative participation methods. A host may stagger starts, but it is **not** real-time manhunt, chase, or an automatic head-to-head multiplayer service. Avoid timed travel incentives.

## 4. School / museum learning

Audience: supervised student or visitor program. An interpretive six-stop lesson with text and non-GPS alternatives. Required: curriculum owner, venue permission, age suitability, adult supervision, languages, mobility considerations, artifact rights and a separate field safety review.

## Content-to-release checklist

- Write the six-stop story, observations, three-hint ladders and fallback activities.
- Verify all factual claims and image/audio rights with a local editor.
- Obtain venue and signage permissions; establish a safe route and accessible alternatives.
- Generate valid SPEC v1 content using quest-authoring. Do not weaken validator rules.
- Run target player checks, device/offline tests and on-location playtests.
- Record a release/version and separate payment/fulfillment verification if sold.
- Only after those gates, consider a city-specific landing page with a unique, useful real route and truthful status.

See the [organizer civic pilot packet](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/organizer-toolkit/blob/main/guides/civic-tourism-pilot.md) and [safe group challenge procedures](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/organizer-toolkit/blob/main/guides/asynchronous-team-challenges.md).

## Example inquiry categories, not pricing or availability

- Existing reviewed edition: ask about present event dates and supported venue.
- Commissioned local story: discovery/feasibility and scoped quote required.
- White-label reuse: future contractual option only, subject to licensing, support, data protection and brand review.

No paid-kit answer keys, private coordinates, customer stories or invented sales results belong in this public concept document.
