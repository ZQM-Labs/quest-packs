# Edition: Wisp's Spring Festival

## ZQM Labs Spring 2027 · March 20 – April 5, 2027

Wisp's spring festival is ready — blossoms, butterflies, streams, and a
singing stone tradition. But six festival items have gone missing, and the
trail goes from sunny to properly strange. A non-Halloween edition for
spring breaks, Earth Day events, and family-friendly outdoor festivals.

## Distribution boundary

This is public catalog documentation only. The full playable pack, answer key, exact route coordinates, QR/sign assets, and organizer materials are delivered privately to authorized organizers and must not be committed to this public repository.

## What's inside the kit

- 6 quest stops, each with a QR sign, a location story, and a puzzle
- 4 Dial levels — **Cozy** (family), **Spooky**, **After Dark**,
  **Overdrive** (office/competitive) — the same stops play differently
  per level
- Hint ladder at every stop (3 hints; the 3rd is the "Ranger assist")
- Printable Letter-size QR signs + start poster (via the kit generator)
- Organizer guide: placement map, answer key, tiebreakers, scoring
- Offline-safe player pages (service worker; no app to install)
